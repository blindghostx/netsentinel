NetSentinel Security

For iPhone and iPad · Luke Turner

NetSentinel is a free Wi-Fi safety and network audit app. Shield scores the Wi-Fi you're on from 0 to 100, explains each finding and what to do about it, and can set up encrypted DNS for your whole device; with Location on, it also warns about open Wi-Fi and look-alike networks. The network audit lists the devices on your Wi-Fi, checks their open ports, explains each device's risk score and exports a PDF report. NetSentinel also flags devices that look like cameras and helps you find Bluetooth trackers that stay near you. Everything runs on your device, with no accounts, analytics, ads or in-app purchases.
